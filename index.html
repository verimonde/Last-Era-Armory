Expert Techniques

Unarmed strike · Three-Form Strike (Expert)

Expert Weapon Proficiency (Unarmed) upgrades the normal 1d4 bludgeoning unarmed strike to 1d6. Choose a separate attack profile for each use: Slashing 1d6, 19–20/x2; Piercing 1d6, x3; Bludgeoning 1d6, x2. Damage type and critical multiplier are chosen before rolling. This replaces Open-Hand Counter.

Gauntlet · Wrestler’s Gauntlet (Expert)

When dealing damage with a gauntlet attack against a creature you are wrestling, add twice your Might modifier to damage in place of the ordinary Might contribution. This does not grant an extra attack.

Push dagger · Clinch Pressure (Expert)

While wrestling a creature and holding the push dagger, deal normal push dagger damage once at the beginning of each of your turns to one creature you are wrestling, without an extra attack roll. When such a creature attempts to escape your wrestling hold, it provokes one attack of opportunity from you with the push dagger if you have an available AoO. This trigger does not grant unlimited AoOs; the automatic damage does not critically hit.

Spiked gauntlet · Barbed Clinch (Expert)

When you successfully initiate wrestling using the spiked-gauntlet hand, deal 1d4 piercing damage to that opponent once, without an extra attack or additional Might damage. You also gain +4 on wrestling checks made to escape a wrestling hold while wearing the gauntlet.

Knife · Hidden Cut (Expert)

If the knife was concealed and the target was unaware of the knife immediately before you drew it, the first Cut against that creature threatens a critical on natural 18–20 and uses x3 damage. This applies to that first Cut only, regardless of whether it hits. The target may know the wielder is present but must not know of the knife.

Club · Batting Return (Expert)

When you are targeted by a physical ranged projectile, you may expend one available attack of opportunity to attempt Batting Return. Make a club attack roll opposed by the incoming ranged attack roll. On success, the projectile is deflected and the incoming attack misses. If the projectile is sufficiently solid, such as a sling stone, you may immediately direct it at another creature within 18 m: make a ranged attack at –4, dealing 1d8 bludgeoning damage plus your Might modifier on a hit. Arrows and bolts may be deflected but cannot be returned as attacks. If the deflection attempt fails, resolve the incoming projectile attack normally. The returned projectile’s critical profile has not been specified; no additional critical mechanic is asserted here.

Light mace · Skull Bash (Expert)

Declare Skull Bash before making a light mace attack against a creature within melee reach. Take –4 to the attack roll. On a hit, deal normal 1d6 bludgeoning damage with a critical profile of 19–20/x2. If the attack deals damage, the target must succeed on a Fortitude save with DC 10 + half the damage dealt (round down) or be deafened for one round per point of your Might modifier (minimum one round). This technique does not change the mace’s free-action draw/stow property.

Heavy mace · Breathbreaker (Expert)

As a standard action, make a heavy mace Strike and take –2 AC until the beginning of your next turn. If the attack deals damage, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + half the damage dealt, rounded down) or become fatigued for 1 round. This does not upgrade fatigue to exhaustion.

Morningstar · Rending Withdrawal (Expert)

After you hit a creature with a morningstar, your next available morningstar attack against that same creature during the same round may be a Rending Withdrawal. On a hit, deal normal 1d8 damage as B + P + S (one damage roll) and replace the normal Might contribution with 1.5× Might one-handed or 2× Might two-handed. It retains the morningstar’s x3 critical. The technique never grants another attack; if there is no other available attack that round, it cannot occur.

Sickle · Ankle Hook (Expert)

Before a sickle trip, forgo the +2 trip bonus. On a successful trip, also deal normal 1d4 slashing sickle damage and reduce the victim’s movement allowance by 3 m (minimum 0). The movement loss persists until a Heal check succeeds with DC equal to the damage dealt by Ankle Hook, or until the victim receives magical healing totaling at least that much restored hit-point damage. If the attack deals 0 damage, it causes no movement loss. Healing/check action costs follow the larger rules.

Shortspear · Deflecting Thrust (Expert)

On a successful adjacent shortspear thrust, forgo all thrust damage to gain +2 AC against that opponent until the beginning of your next turn. This does not grant another attack or affect attacks from other opponents.

Longspear · Shortened Thrust (Expert)

By shortening the grip, you may make the longspear’s normal Thrust attack against an adjacent opponent at 1.5 m using its full 1d8 piercing damage, x3 critical, and normal attack modifiers. There is no damage or attack penalty. The same thrust may otherwise be used at 3 m. Switching grip requires no action.

Spear · Bash and Point (Expert)

If your spear Butt Strike damages an opponent, your next available spear Thrust against that opponent during the same round gains +2 on its attack roll and may be made at adjacent range. Bash and Point uses the spear’s normal 1d8 piercing Thrust and x3 critical. It does not grant an additional attack; the preceding Butt Strike must deal damage. The ordinary spear already has adjacent reach, so this clause does not extend its range.

Dart · Twin-Dart Fan (Expert)

Expend two darts against two creatures within 1.5 m of each other, both within thrown range. Make one attack roll at –2 and compare it with each AC; each hit deals 1d4 piercing damage. Each potential critical must be confirmed separately against its target, using the dart’s normal x3 critical. Each target receives applicable damage modifiers once.

Javelin · Retreating Jab (Expert)

When a creature moves from outside your melee reach into an adjacent square, you may expend an available attack of opportunity to make a melee javelin thrust against it. If you hit, you may immediately step up to 1.5 m directly away without provoking an AoO from that creature; other opponents threaten normally. This is an alternative melee technique and does not replace Shield Pin on thrown attacks.

Sling · Overhand Release (Expert)

Spend one standard action to fire a single sling stone or bullet dealing 2d6 base bludgeoning damage. The attack retains the sling’s normal x2 critical possibility and adds eligible damage modifiers once. All ordinary range and ammunition rules apply.

Crossbow, light · Close Release (Expert)

Fire an already-loaded light crossbow at an adjacent enemy, taking –4 on the attack roll. This shot does not provoke an attack of opportunity from that target; other enemies may threaten normally under the standard rules. Close Release cannot Overpenetrate. Use the crossbow’s ordinary 1d10 piercing damage and x3 critical. This does not grant a ranged attack of opportunity.

Crossbow, heavy · Vital Shot (Expert)

Declare Vital Shot before firing a loaded heavy crossbow as a standard action. Roll its 2d8 base damage twice (4d8 total base piercing damage) against the one struck creature. Add ordinary damage modifiers once, and resolve the normal x3 critical rules. The bolt cannot Overpenetrate. Reloading still requires a standard action.

Hand axe · Bearded Lock (Expert)

When your hand axe deals damage to a target, you may catch its guarding weapon with the hooked head. That target cannot make attacks of opportunity against you while you remain adjacent to it, until the beginning of your next turn. The effect ends immediately if you cease to be adjacent; it affects no other enemy.

Throwing axe · Flying Sunder (Expert)

You may attempt a sunder against a held item with a thrown axe. Resolve one ranged attack against the item at –2 under your sunder rules and deal normal throwing-axe damage to the item, applying object hardness; no damage is dealt to the holder. No extra attack is generated.

Light hammer · Armor Sunder (Expert)

You may target armor currently being worn with a light hammer sunder maneuver. Make an ordinary sunder attack against the worn armor, using the combat system’s object defense and hardness rules; a success damages the armor, not its wearer. This creates no attack or damage bonus against any armor category and does not bypass object hardness.

Kukri · Turning Slice (Expert)

When a normal kukri Slash misses a creature, you may redirect the same attack at a different enemy also adjacent to you and within reach. Compare the original attack result at –2 against the second target’s AC. On a hit, deal 1d4 slashing damage plus normal eligible modifiers. This is the same swing, not an additional attack; use the kukri’s normal critical rules, confirming separately against the second target if its die threatens.

Light pick · Gravebreaker Point (Expert)

A light pick may score and confirm its normal x4 critical hits against corporeal Undead and corporeal Artifice creatures even if their creature type normally makes them immune to critical hits. This does not affect incorporeal creatures, immunity from other independent sources, or objects unless your rules allow a critical against that object.

Sap · Concussive Silence (Expert)

When your sap deals at least 1 nonlethal damage to a creature, you may cause it to be unable to speak or shout for a number of rounds equal to your positive Might modifier (minimum 0 rounds). Effects requiring speech or audible verbal components cannot be used during this duration. This does not prevent ordinary movement or attacks.

Spiked armor · Spiked Push-Over (Expert)

When you successfully perform a push-over involving body contact while wearing spiked armor, you may also deal the armor’s normal 1d6 piercing damage to that opponent. Resolve the push-over normally; the additional armor damage needs no second attack roll and adds the normal permitted damage modifiers once. If no body contact occurs, this effect does not trigger.

Arming sword · Binding Turn (Expert)

Declare Binding Turn against an armed opponent and make an arming sword Slash at –8 to attack. If the attack hits, it deals normal 1d8 slashing damage and uses a 17–20/x2 critical profile. If the attack misses and the natural attack die was not a 1, immediately attempt a disarm check against that opponent with +4 to the disarm check; use the campaign’s normal disarm rules. A natural 1 grants no disarm attempt. The technique does not grant another damaging attack and does not require a shield or another weapon.

Battleaxe · Buried Edge (Expert)

Once per turn, declare Buried Edge before a battleaxe Chop. On a hit, deal normal slashing damage, and the axe becomes embedded in the target. The target gains bleed points equal to the attacker’s Might modifier. An embedded axe cannot be used for other attacks until removed with a successful DC 16 Might check. Removal does not stop bleeding; use the Bleeding rule. Normal axe Might and Mighty Blow rules apply.

Cestus · Rend (Expert)

If two separate Cestus attacks hit the same opponent during the same round, you may immediately make one additional Rend attack against that opponent. Rend deals 1d6 slashing damage, threatens on 19–20/x2, and uses ordinary Might damage. Once per round; Rend does not count toward triggering another Rend. The two original hits need not come from separate gloves.

Dagger · Pierce and Twist (Expert)

As a standard action, make a dagger Thrust at –2 attack. On a hit, deal normal 1d6 piercing damage and 1d4 additional slashing damage from twisting the point. Apply Might once, to the piercing component; only the piercing component uses the x3 critical multiplier. The extra slashing die cannot critically multiply.

Heavy pick · Gravebreaker Point (Expert)

A heavy pick may score and confirm its normal x4 critical hits against corporeal Undead and corporeal Artifice creatures even if their creature type normally makes them immune to critical hits. This does not affect incorporeal creatures, immunity from other independent sources, or objects unless your rules allow a critical against that object.

Scimitar · Flowing Cut (Expert)

After a successful Draw Cut, your next available scimitar attack before the end of the turn may target a different opponent adjacent to the first. It gains +2 attack. Flowing Cut grants no extra attack and the +2 does not stack with another Flowing Cut; only one such bonus may affect an attack roll.

Warhammer · Hammer and Break (Expert)

As a standard action, strike one opponent with the warhammer. On a hit, deal the normal damage of the chosen hammer or back-point mode to that opponent and also deal the same attack’s weapon damage to one held weapon or shield belonging to that opponent, subject to that object’s hardness and normal sunder rules. Resolve the object damage separately, with no second attack roll, and do not double-apply Might to either single damage roll.

Shield, light · Covering Bash (Expert)

On a successful light-shield bash, forgo the bash damage to extend your shield-derived AC bonus to one adjacent ally against the next melee attack before your next turn. You lose that shield-derived AC bonus for the same duration. The bonus is transferred, not duplicated.

Shield, light spiked · Riposte Spike (Expert)

After an adjacent opponent misses you with a melee attack, expend an available attack of opportunity to attack that opponent with your light shield spike at –2. Lose your shield-derived AC bonus until the start of your next turn. This is a spike attack, not a counter-damage effect.

Shield, heavy · Shield Press (Expert)

Make a heavy-shield bash using 1d4 bludgeoning base damage in place of its normal 1d6. If it hits, you may attempt to shove the target using the same attack roll, without a separate attack; if successful, move it 1.5 m away and step into its vacated square if legal. Apply eligible Might damage to the bash once.

Shield, heavy spiked · Riposte Spike (Expert)

After an adjacent opponent misses you with a melee attack, expend an available attack of opportunity to attack that opponent with your heavy shield spike at –2. Lose your shield-derived AC bonus until the start of your next turn. This is a spike attack, not a counter-damage effect.

Greataxe · Falling Chop (Expert)

Make a Greataxe Chop at –2 to attack, dealing the greataxe’s normal 1d12 slashing damage with normal criticals, axe Might and Mighty Blow rules. If the attack misses its first target, you may immediately redirect the same swing against a different adjacent enemy within reach, making a second attack roll at –2. The redirected swing does not occur if the first attack hit. When a Falling Chop hits a Plant-type creature, add one additional 1d12 weapon damage die (2d12 total base damage against that creature). Apply normal damage modifiers once per hit; the extra die follows the ordinary critical profile. No more than one secondary target may be attacked.

Greatclub · Rolling Impact (Expert)

After a Greatclub attack deals damage, you may immediately attempt a free push-over against that target with a +2 bonus. Make the push-over check separately, without using another action or attack. The original strike retains its damage.

Scythe · Reaping Arc (Expert)

As a standard action, designate three contiguous 1.5 m squares adjacent to you that lie within your scythe’s reach. Make one Slashing Cut attack roll at –2 and compare it against every creature in those squares, up to one per square. Each creature hit takes normal 2d4 slashing damage and applicable damage modifiers. Any threatening critical is confirmed separately for each hit target with the scythe’s normal 19–20/x3 profile.

Shortbow · Mounted Release / Close-Quarters Shot (Expert)

Mounted Release: When mounted on a moving steed, reduce the ordinary attack penalty imposed by mounted archery by 2 (minimum 0); it does not remove other attack penalties. Close-Quarters Shot: You may fire a shortbow at an adjacent creature at –2 to attack without provoking an attack of opportunity from that targeted creature; other threatening opponents may still make AoOs. Both options require Expert proficiency in the Shortbow.

Longbow · Measured Release (Expert)

Choose the time commitment before beginning a single longbow attack: one ordinary available attack gives no precision bonus; one standard-action shot gains +2 to attack; one full-round action gains +4; a one-round aiming action that finishes immediately before the beginning of your next turn gains +6. Only the highest applicable bonus applies to that shot; they do not add together. Any movement, attack, or interruption during the aim forfeits the pending bonus. It remains a single arrow attack and normal cover/range rules apply.

Kama · Turning Hook (Expert)

Before a kama trip, forgo its +2 trip bonus. If you trip a creature no larger than your own size, you may exchange positions with it before it falls prone, provided both spaces are legal and have room. This works only against same-size or smaller creatures.

Nunchaku · Catch and Claim (Expert)

Before disarming with a nunchaku, forgo its +2 disarm bonus. On success, catch the released item in your empty off-hand if it can be held there; otherwise the item drops normally. No additional disarm attack is granted.

Sai · Concussive Side Strike (Expert)

Use the sai’s flat-bar strike to make a 1d4 bludgeoning attack (x2). If that attack deals damage, you may forgo the Might contribution to its damage to impose –2 on the target’s next melee attack before the beginning of your next turn. The sai’s ordinary thrust remains piercing, and the side of the sai remains usable as a normal bludgeoning mode without this Expert effect.

Siangham · Aim and Plow (Expert)

The siangham now has two normal attack profiles: Slash, 1d6 slashing with a 19–20/x2 critical; and Thrust, 1d6 piercing with an x3 critical. If a Slash hits and its 1d6 weapon damage die shows 1 or 2, your next available Thrust against the same opponent during the same round gains +3 flat damage on a hit. This bonus does not stack, is not multiplied on a critical hit, and does not grant an additional attack. The trigger checks only the Slash weapon die, not Might or other added damage.

Whip · Lash Draw (Expert)

When a whip attack hits a target no more than one size category larger than you, you may forgo its Might damage modifier to attempt a pull using that same attack roll as the maneuver roll. On success, draw the target 1.5 m directly toward you into an unoccupied space. The attack still deals its other eligible damage, and the normal nonlethal rules apply.

Crescent sword · Scything Circuit (Expert)

As a standard action, make one 2d4 slashing attack with each end of the Crescent Sword against two different adjacent opponents, taking –2 on each attack roll. If both attacks hit, you may immediately move 1.5 m without provoking attacks of opportunity from either struck opponent. This movement is in addition to your normal movement allowance and consumes no movement from it. Other enemies threaten normally. The attacks retain the normal 19–20/x2 critical profile. Scything Circuit provides exactly two attacks as a special standard action; it does not add other attacks.

Spiked chain · Wide Chain Sweep (Expert)

As a standard action, swing the chain in one contiguous 90-degree arc reaching up to 3 m from you. Make one attack roll at –2 and compare it against the AC of up to three creatures within that arc. Each hit deals 2d4 bludgeoning and piercing damage (one roll per target), with normal eligible damage modifiers and normal x3 critical confirmation per target. Bludgeoning bypasses DR but not object hardness. The chain retains normal adjacent/3 m range for its ordinary single-target attacks.

Bolas · Limb Snare (Expert)

Declare Limb Snare before throwing bolas. Take –4 on the ranged attack and select one of the target’s arms. On a hit, that arm becomes entangled and cannot be used until the target spends a standard action freeing it. The bolas deal no damage and do not trip. The attack uses the bolas’ ordinary thrown range.

Crossbow, hand · Sleeve Shot (Expert)

If a hand crossbow is concealed and loaded before the attack, draw and fire it in melee as a single attack without provoking an attack of opportunity from the targeted opponent. Other creatures still threaten and may make AoOs normally. The hand crossbow uses normal bolt damage and Overpenetration.

Net · Hold Fast (Expert)

After successfully entangling a target with a net, you may retain its tether. While you maintain the tether, that target cannot move farther than 3 m from you unless it wins an opposed Wrestling check. Holding the tether occupies one hand. You may release the tether without spending an action; releasing it ends this particular restriction but does not automatically end the net’s ordinary entanglement.

Shuriken · Disengaging Flick (Expert)

Declare Disengaging Flick before throwing a shuriken. If the throw hits, forgo all shuriken damage and make a Bluff check opposed by the target’s Sense Motive. If the target fails its opposed Sense Motive check, it cannot make attacks of opportunity until the beginning of your next turn. If it succeeds, the effect fails and no damage is dealt. A miss never triggers the opposed check.

Blowgun · Deep Inoculation (Expert)

When a blowgun dart delivers a poison that deals a minimum amount of poison damage on a successful saving throw, increase that minimum by +1. Example: a 2d6 Agility-damage poison that ordinarily deals minimum 2 after a save deals 3. The effect does not increase damage when the saving throw fails, bypass immunity, change the poison’s DC, or affect a poison that has no minimum-on-save damage.

Reference appendix • A

Weapon-family rules

Bleeding

A creature loses hit points each round equal to its total stacked bleed points. A successful Heal check (DC 10 + 2 per bleed point) stops bleeding. Magical healing removes 1 bleed point for every 5 points of healing, applied to bleeding first; remaining healing restores hit points. Removing an embedded weapon does not stop bleeding.

Shared mechanics printed in the weapon tables are defined here.

Expert Weapon Proficiency (weapon)

Talent, weapon-specific: Choose one weapon. You are proficient with it (no –4 nonproficiency attack penalty) and may use its (Expert) attacks and effects. Choose this talent separately for each weapon. Simple or Martial proficiency alone does not permit (Expert) techniques. For an Expert-category weapon, this talent grants both basic proficiency and its tagged techniques. Unmarked attacks and weapon properties follow normal proficiency rules.

Axes: force through a cutting head

Axe-head/Mighty Blow applies to an axe-head melee cutting attack, including martial hand axes, battleaxes, greataxes, the axe blade of a halberd. It does not apply to a shaft/haft strike, a spear end, or a thrown axe.

  • Add 1.5 × Might modifier to damage when using the axe head in one hand.
  • Add 2 × Might modifier to damage when using the axe head with two-handed leverage.
  • When using Mighty Blow on an eligible axe-head attack, add +1 damage on top of Mighty Blow’s normal benefit except for Greataxe Chop and Halberd Chop.

Heavy-axe Mighty Blow exception, Greataxe Chop and Halberd Chop only: Mighty Blow converts each point of attack penalty taken into +3 damage when used with two-handed leverage (normally +2), or +1.5 damage when legitimately wielded one-handed (normally +1). For example, a –2 attack penalty grants +6 damage in two hands. The one-handed case only applies if the wielder is otherwise allowed to use that weapon in one hand. This replaces, and does not stack with, the general axe family's extra +1 Mighty Blow damage. It modifies Mighty Blow damage, not the separate Might ability modifier.

The underlying axe-head Might damage bonus remains 1.5 × Might one-handed or 2 × Might two-handed. This rule includes off-hand axe-head attacks. Never count an off-hand cut as two-handed leverage merely because the item has two ends. Apply your campaign’s ordinary rounding rule to fractional damage. The battleaxe preserves its one-handed Mighty Blow grip interaction below. Mighty Blow remains your Power Attack-style option; its attack penalties and activation follow your combat rules.

Spears: bracing against a charge

Shortspear, spear, and longspear have x2 damage when a readied thrust hits a charging enemy. A halberd’s top spike also explicitly has that property. A lance has a different trigger: the wielder charges while mounted. Readying, charging and critical multipliers otherwise follow your combat system. The former arbitrary +2 to attack or +2 damage on spear brace is removed.

Bows: close cover and Might-rated strings

Every bow ignores all cover-derived AC bonuses and cover-derived attack penalties when shooting a target within or at 9 m, so long as that cover is less than total/full cover. It requires no extra action and has no attack penalty. Total/full cover still prevents the shot. A bow does not ignore concealment, darkness, armor, or any defense unrelated to cover. There is no High-Arc Shot mode.

Might-rated strings: Every Shortbow and Longbow must use a string rated +0 or higher. A +N string requires a Might score of 10 + 2N (Might modifier at least +N), and adds +N arrow damage on a successful hit. Below the minimum Might score the string cannot be used. A stronger archer receives no extra bow damage above the string rating; do not also add the Might modifier. The string does not change the bow’s base dice, range or proficiency. Example: a +2 string requires Might 14 and adds +2 damage.

Crossbows: Overpenetration

When a solid crossbow bolt hits its first creature and inflicts at least 1 point of damage after DR and reductions, it continues along the same firing line and can hit one other creature directly behind the first. The second creature must lie on the original straight bolt trajectory, within range, and not behind total/full cover. Make a new attack roll at –2 against that second creature. On a hit, roll normal bolt damage and halve the resulting damage (round down). The second hit cannot critically hit or pass through another creature. No more than two creatures can be damaged by one bolt. Other occupants not directly behind the first creature are unaffected.

An attack against the second creature is possible only when the first creature actually took damage. Only solid crossbow bolts can Overpenetrate. The heavy crossbow deals 2d8 piercing and requires one standard action to reload.

Shields: javelin obstruction

Shield pin is specific to a javelin. If a thrown javelin misses a shield-bearing creature and the attack die was not a natural 1, the shield provides no shield-derived benefits until the end of that creature’s next turn. The javelin is considered lodged in or obstructing the shield. It does not make the shield physically disappear or count as a successful damaging hit. A second pin before recovery does not stack extra penalties; use the latest applicable end time. Full/total cover still prevents a legal attack, and the rule requires a legal attack roll at the creature. A creature is shield-bearing only if it has the shield equipped and active when targeted.

Specialized polearm maneuvers (Expert)

Guisarme, Billhook, Halberd and Ranseur may sacrifice their listed +4 maneuver bonus before rolling. If that maneuver succeeds, apply the named extra effect with no second attack roll. A failed maneuver has no extra effect.

Drawing Hook drags the target up to 1.5 m toward the guisarme wielder before prone. Rending Hook deals Billhook Hook Cut damage in addition to a trip. Destructive Chop deals normal Halberd Chop damage to the wielder of the sundered object; object damage remains normal. Wrenching Thrust deals normal Ranseur Thrust damage to the disarmed opponent.

Flails: Entangling Critical (Expert)

On a confirmed critical with a flexible flail head, attempt one immediate free disarm against that same opponent, without provoking an attack of opportunity. Use a normal disarm roll but replace the Might contribution with Might modifier × the weapon critical multiplier. Retain the original critical-hit damage. Shaft strikes do not trigger this technique. Applies to Flail, Heavy flail.

Battlefield techniques (Expert)

Quarterstaff Covering Strike: declare before attacking. On a hit, forgo the Might modifier to damage; the target cannot make attacks of opportunity until the start of the wielder’s next turn. Other damage bonuses apply normally.

Trident Forked Bind: forgo +2 disarm. On a successful disarm, trap the weapon rather than dropping it. Any held weapon eligible for disarm may be bound by catching the weapon, its handle, or its wielder’s arm. The opponent cannot attack or make attacks of opportunity, and the trident wielder cannot attack or make attacks of opportunity with the trident, until released.

The trapped opponent may replace an attack with a counter-disarm. Each failure deals normal trident thrust damage. Abandoning the trapped weapon ends the bind and deals normal trident thrust damage once; a successful counter-disarm or voluntary release by the trident wielder ends it without further damage. No additional attack roll or critical hit applies to this automatic damage.

Short sword Inside the Guard: after a missed attack from 3 m, spend an available attack of opportunity to move 1.5 m toward the attacker and thrust if now within reach. Glaive Passing Cut: reduce base cutting damage to 1d6; on a hit move 1.5 m without provoking an attack of opportunity from the struck target. Lance Ride-Through: on a successful mounted charge, forgo x2 charge damage and push the target 1.5 m along the direction of travel if there is room.

Concealment Visual concealment, such as darkness or smoke, is distinct from physical cover.
Adjacent / 3 m Adjacent normally means 1.5 m. A polearm with 3 m reach normally cannot use that attack at 1.5 m unless a separate attack mode permits it.
Thrown / range increment Thrown N m is a thrown range increment; an unqualified ranged distance is a projectile range increment. Attacks past the first increment take your standard range penalties (normally –2 per extra increment). Maximum range remains governed by your game.
Light A light weapon may use Might or Agility for its attack modifier, as defined in your prior rule. Damage modifiers still use the normal damage rules unless a specialty says otherwise.
Might / Agility / Legerdemain Character modifiers and the concealing-objects skill in your own system.
Mighty Blow Your Power Attack-type option, trading attack accuracy for extra damage. Most axe-head attacks gain +1 bonus damage when using it; Greataxe Chop and Halberd Chop use the Mighty Blow exchange-rate exception in Weapon-family rules, with no additional +1. The activation rules come from your combat system.
Free / move / standard / full-round action The action categories used by your combat system. Free actions take no action slot on your turn; move and standard actions consume their respective slots; full-round actions occupy the full-round slot. Where your action system differs, translate the category before play.
Draw / stow Retrieve the item into the hand / return it to its holder or sheath. Stowing requires a suitable holder and a free hand or normal way of securing it.
Readied attack / charge A readied attack is prepared in advance and triggers on its specified event. A charge is the campaign’s special committed attack movement. An attack readied against a charge must strike the charging creature when the trigger occurs.
Attack of opportunity A reactive attack triggered by the combat rules. Arming sword and bastard sword Thrusts each gain +2 to attack when used for an attack of opportunity.
Coup de Jarnac A rapier-specific counterthrust triggered immediately after an opponent misses the wielder with a melee attack. The attack uses +2 to hit and 18–20/x3. The number of reactive attacks permitted and the action cost are governed by your broader combat rules; the counter does not grant unlimited free attacks.
Expert Weapon Proficiency (Unarmed) A talent that changes unarmed strike damage to 1d6 and permits choosing bludgeoning, piercing or slashing per attack; use the normal critical profile for that chosen type. Expert is part of the talent name and does not change the strike’s Simple proficiency classification.
Trip / disarm / shove / sunder Combat maneuvers to knock prone, remove a held weapon, push a target, or damage an object. Listed maneuver bonuses modify the relevant maneuver roll, not ordinary weapon-attack damage. A maneuver consumes the usual attack or action, and succeeds under your broader maneuver rules.
Specialized maneuver / sacrifice bonusThe wielder forgoes the printed bonus before a maneuver roll; only a successful maneuver gains the listed extra effect. No extra attack or attack roll.

Reference appendix • C

Reading the catalog

Proficiency categories are Simple, Martial, and Expert. A nonproficient user has –4 to attack. The Expert Weapon Proficiency (specific weapon) talent removes that penalty for its named weapon and unlocks all (Expert) techniques. Ordinary category proficiency alone never unlocks marked techniques. Expert-category weapons use the same talent for basic proficiency.

Each physical weapon is shown once. Alternate attack modes, including named specialized maneuvers, appear below that weapon. Techniques never grant extra attacks unless a rule expressly states otherwise.

Damage baseline for newly designed melee weapons: simple light 1d4, one-handed 1d6, two-handed 1d8; martial/expert light 1d6, one-handed 1d8, two-handed 1d10. Existing user-specified dice and physical specialization override that baseline. Increased dice, exceptional critical profiles, weapon handling and combat maneuvers all draw from the same general power budget. Bows, crossbows, thrown missiles and double weapons follow their listed family profiles.

Equipment-neutral specialization rule: A weapon does not gain an attack or damage bonus simply because an opponent wears a particular armor type, carries a particular weapon, or uses a shield. Apply normal AC, DR and combat-maneuver rules. A weapon may still have a distinct, explicitly defined interaction such as the javelin’s Shield pin, which is a specific obstruction effect requested for that weapon, not a generic damage or accuracy bonus against shields. Object hardness remains separate from DR. Weapon bonuses should come from grip, attack timing, leverage, reach, maneuver technique, damage profile or similar properties of the attack itself.

  • S: Slashing, normally critical 19–20/x2.
  • P: Piercing, normally critical 20/x3.
  • B: Bludgeoning, normally critical 20/x2 and bypasses damage reduction (DR).
  • B + P: One attack that has both damage types at once, not two attacks and not two damage rolls. It inherits bludgeoning DR bypass. Its printed critical profile governs its criticals; the morningstar’s profile is x3. The original flail follows the same interpretation.
  • B + S + P: A mixed-damage attack. It is a single damage roll using all three types and its printed critical profile.

The printed critical profile always takes precedence over a type’s default. Specialized blades may have 18–20/x2; specialized picks may have x4. Mixed damage never grants multiple critical rolls or multiple simultaneous multipliers. Where a resistance or vulnerability concerns a type, treat a combined attack as having all of its listed types and resolve its damage once using the campaign’s ordinary resistance rules. No extra damage is created merely by listing two types.
